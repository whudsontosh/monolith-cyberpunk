# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Netrunning, from the Cyberpunk 2020 rulebook's Netrunner chapter.

- **Your deck:** a Netrunning tab on the sheet with your cyberdeck (the book's decks, from the stock deck
  to the Parraline 5750 and the Kirama LPD-12), how you connect (plugs, 'trodes or keyboard), options,
  and upgrades at the book's prices: +1 Speed, +1 data walls, a second memory.
- **Programs:** every program in the book, from Hammer to Hellhound, with what it does. Load programs into
  your deck's memory or keep them on chips, and carry subroutines in Demons (half the memory, the Demon's
  Strength).
- **Net rolls:** Net initiative, log off, and a Net roll window for every program class, using the book's
  formulas, with the result tagged in the roll log.
- **Writing programs:** the Programming 101 rules, with Difficulty, size, work time and street value, and the
  INT + Programming check.
- **Data fortresses (GM):** build systems on the 20 × 20 subgrid: data walls, code gates, CPUs, memories with
  files and virtual realities, remotes, LDLs and defences. INT, AI, actions, memory and construction cost
  are worked out as you build, and the rulebook's Fast Fortress tables roll one for you.
- **Live runs:** start a run and the players' runners jack in. Each player sees the subgrid from their
  runner's position, with line of sight and explored areas, and moves and runs programs from the Net
  window. The GM's app rolls both sides: walls, gates, stealth and detection, Anti-IC, anti-personnel
  damage that lands on the runner's wounds, anti-system attacks on decks, controllers, reading and copying
  files. The GM runs the defences, initiative and turns, and can act for runners who aren't connected.
- Homebrew packs can add programs, ICE and decks. The campaign shop can hide or reprice them.
  Campaign packages carry data fortresses.
- The paper sheet gets a cyberdeck and programs page, and the rules reference a Netrunning section.
- Added the Cybermodem link (neuralware) from the cyberware chart.

## 1.0.0

Cyberpunk 2020 for Monolith: everything from Night City Dossier, now as a game Monolith installs and keeps up to date.

- Build runners with the guided or quick builder, keep the full sheet, buy gear with item explanations,
  make skill checks and attacks with hit locations, damage and ammo.
- The GM console: the party, NPCs and templates, the encounter tracker with initiative and damage,
  awards, the rules reference and printable sheets and NPC cards.
- Homebrew packs (`.cp2020homebrew`), campaign packages (`.cp2020campaign`), the campaign shop and the shop lock.
- The Night City look, on every screen of the game.
